ElevenHouse — RU/EN localization review, 2026-10-16

Implemented
- One persistent language preference for authentication, onboarding, workspace, client portal and administration.
- Language selection in account menu and profile settings; language controls on standalone surfaces.
- Translated navigation, forms, feedback, empty states, guided learning, calculations, statuses and accessible names.
- Locale-aware dates, weekdays, numbers and plurals. Currency remains RUB; changing language does not change monetary amounts.
- Browser constraint validation uses the selected product language.
- Client names, notes, conversations, service descriptions and other authored content remain unchanged.
- Switching language does not remount the workspace or reset open drafts.
- Account menu fits narrow and short windows. Active language keeps contrast on hover.
